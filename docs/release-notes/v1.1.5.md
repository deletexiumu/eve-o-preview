EVE-O Preview for macOS 1.1.5 makes hotkey scopes easier to read.

## Improved

- **Clearer hotkey scope:** each hotkey works in exactly one scope. "Global" works in every app, including EVE, and "Only in EVE" works only while an EVE window is in front. The scope choice on the Clients page and the default scope on the Switching & Hotkeys page are now two radio buttons that say this, instead of a two-part switch that looked as if both could be on.
- **Scope shown on every hotkey:** on the Switching & Hotkeys page, each hotkey now shows its scope as an icon and a name, such as "Only in EVE", with a menu arrow. Click it to change the scope of that one hotkey. Before, only an icon was shown.

Hotkeys work as before. Your settings do not change.

## Upgrading

From 1.1.0 or later, the update installs from inside the app: automatically when no EVE client has been running for about a minute, when you quit, or from "Check for Updates…". From 1.0.0, download `EVE-O-Preview-1.1.5.dmg` and replace the app once. Screen Recording and Accessibility stay granted.

This build is signed with the project's own certificate and is not notarized. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of all files.
